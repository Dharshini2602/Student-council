How it works, step by step

Log in, share what matters and follow what happens next. Four simple steps, one clear promise.

Every idea and every message starts with you. Here is exactly what happens after you log in and tap send.

A reply in a week, a plan soon after.

  1. Step 1

    Login

    Sign in with your college mail ID (@ppg.edu.in). Your student account keeps every message private.

  2. Step 2

    Share

    Post a suggestion or ask for support. Tell us as much or as little as you like.

  3. Step 3

    Review

    The council team reads every message and discusses it at our next meeting.

  4. Step 4

    Action

    We act on it, follow up with you and update everyone on what changed.

A first reply usually lands within a week.

A team of elected students, one shared table and a promise to show up for every voice on campus.

Before you ask

The questions we hear most from students. If yours is not here, write to us and we will help.

Who can log in?

Only students with a valid college mail ID ending in @ppg.edu.in can log in. Use that address to enter, and please never share your password or login details with anyone.

Is my mental-health message really confidential?

Yes. Your message goes only to the council team and stays private. We listen first and never share your details without permission.

How do I send a suggestion?

Log in with your college mail, open Student Suggestions and write your idea. You can send it with your name or anonymously, and we read every one.

How can I join the Student Council?

Watch the Upcoming section for our yearly intake. Applications usually open before the new term, and every student is welcome to apply.

Where can I see upcoming and past events?

The Upcoming section lists what is next, and Events holds our past celebrations. If you need help finding one, contact us.

Let's talk. We listen.

Tell us what is on your mind. We reply within two working days, and every message is read by a real student.
